Before you start

  • The viva lasts 15 minutes. The timer is always on screen.
  • The examiner asks each question out loud and on screen. Answer by speaking or typing, in English, Hindi or a mix.
  • Each answer can be up to about 90 seconds. It's fine to say "I don't know"; the examiner simply moves on.
  • Stay on this tab. Switching tabs, long silences and pasted text are noted for your examiner.
  • The examiner won't reveal answers or marks. Marks come after the viva and are checked by faculty.
  • If you get disconnected, sign in again with the same email and viva code. The clock keeps running.
